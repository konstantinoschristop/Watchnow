Every streaming service now has a page of its own.

• In Browse by Streaming, tap Explore all to see everything on a service: its Top 10, Movies or Series, every genre, sorted by Popular, Top Rated or Newest.
• Search within a service, and see where anything it doesn’t have is streaming instead. Your own services now come first.
• A new header on Movies and Series: each title’s own logo over a still from it, with Trailer and Watch Later right there.
• More titles have trailers, and going back to your Watchlist is smoother.
