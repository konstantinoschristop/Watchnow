Watchnow is a free watchlist for movies and series that shows you where to stream them and tells you when they air. No account needed — your watchlist stays on your devices and in your iCloud.

YOUR WATCHLIST
• Save movies and series with one tap
• Organize into folders — Date Night, Halloween, Sci-Fi Marathon
• Mark titles watched and keep track of what you've seen
• Every saved poster shows where you can stream it
• Syncs across your devices with iCloud

WHERE TO WATCH
• See which services have a title in your region
• Compare streaming, rental and purchase options
• Jump straight to the service you want
• Powered by JustWatch

NEVER MISS WHAT'S NEXT
• Save a series and get told when the next episode airs
• Get told when something on your list lands on a service you have
• While You Were Away catches you up on new trailers, episodes and streaming arrivals

MOVIE NIGHT
• Can't agree? Pick a mood, pass the phone, swipe to a match
• Tuned to the services you actually have

MOVIE COACH
• A personal second opinion on any movie or series
• Ask "Is it slow?" or "Is it worth the runtime?"
• Learns from what you save and like
• Runs entirely on your device with Apple's Foundation Models
• Requires an Apple Intelligence–capable iPhone (iPhone 15 Pro or newer) on iOS 26 or later

DISCOVER
• Trending, in theaters and coming soon, refreshed daily
• Browse by genre or by your streaming services
• Cast and crew with filmographies, trailers, reviews, seasons and episodes
• Search movies, series and actors instantly

DESIGNED FOR iOS
• Native SwiftUI, dark mode, smooth animations
• VoiceOver, larger text and Reduce Motion supported
• Available in English, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French and Greek
• Your watchlist and preferences stay on your devices and in your iCloud

Movie and TV data provided by TMDB. Streaming availability provided by JustWatch.
